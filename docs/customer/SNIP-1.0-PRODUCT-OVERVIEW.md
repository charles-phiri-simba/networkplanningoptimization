# SNIP 1.0 — Product overview

SNIP 1.0 is an **AI-assisted** network intelligence, assurance, investigation, planning and optimization platform with **human-governed change rehearsal**.

It is a controlled **synthetic demonstration** of that journey. It is **not** production-ready, **not** production-certified, and **not** a live-network write system.

## What SNIP 1.0 demonstrates

- Network Operations over a synthetic radio-access inventory
- Rule-based Assurance findings with evidence
- Investigation of cell, site, neighbour, and KPI context
- Bounded what-if **txPower** planning against a **cell Digital Twin**
- Deterministic, evidence-backed single-cell optimization proposals
- Human review, approval, change planning, and readiness
- Sandbox rehearsal on `snip-simulator` only
- Four-way state that keeps the **real network unchanged**

## What it is not

SNIP 1.0 does not provide autonomous AI optimization, self-healing, predictive RF, coverage or interference simulation, a site or network Digital Twin, joint multi-cell optimization, live Ericsson or Nokia writes, or Huawei support.

Ask SNIP is decision support. Default generation is a stub with lexical retrieval, not a production telecom LLM.

## Next commercial stage

A **read-only customer PoC** on real inventory, configuration, and KPIs — still without production writes.
